# HaploBreedR 0.99.0

## New features

* Initial submission to Bioconductor.
* `haplobreed_analysis()` identifies superior haplotypes at candidate
  genes from GWAS hits and genotype data.
* `hbr_breeding_decision()` produces multi-trait parent rankings, cross
  recommendations, and a written report.
* Publication-quality visualization of haplotypes, gene models, and LD
  structure.
* GO enrichment, cross-annotation between MSU7 and RAP-DB, and
  manuscript-ready Excel workbooks.
